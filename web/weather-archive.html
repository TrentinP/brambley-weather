About the record. The historical spreadsheet archive begins on 15 January 2020. Some dates or individual measurements are absent in the source record and are intentionally left blank rather than estimated. Earlier records contain the core daily temperature, rainfall and wind measurements. Additional pressure, humidity, UV and solar fields appear where the source record supports them. Historical solar values are station observations and should not be treated as a calibrated photovoltaic site assessment.
